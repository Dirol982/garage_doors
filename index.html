    Before you call

    How fast can you get here?

    Most calls in Tomball, Spring, Cypress, Magnolia and The Woodlands are seen the same day. Emergency calls (a door stuck open at night, a car trapped inside) are answered 24/7.

    How much is a broken spring?

    Most homes pay $250–$450 to replace both torsion springs. A heavy 18-foot or wood door costs more. We always replace the pair, because the second spring is the same age and usually fails within months.

    Can I open the door with a broken spring?

    Please don't. The spring carries the weight of the door, often 150–250 lb. Without it the opener strains, and lifting it by hand can drop it on someone. Leave it closed and text us a photo.

    Can you give me a price by text?

    Yes. Text (832) 606-6633 a photo of the whole door from inside, plus one of the springs above it. For most repairs that's enough to tell you the problem and the price.

    Which brands do you work on?

    All the major door and opener brands you'll find in Texas homes, including LiftMaster, Chamberlain, Genie, Clopay and Amarr. Older and builder-grade doors too.

    Repair it or replace it?

    If the panels are straight and the door is under about 15 years old, repair is almost always the better deal. If panels are cracked, rusted through or the door is uninsulated in a garage you use, we'll show you both prices side by side.

    Do I need a wind-rated door?

    Hurricane winds push the garage door in first, and once it fails the roof is next. We install wind-rated doors and can tell you what your builder or HOA requires. Read our guide.

    Do you do commercial doors?

    Yes: storefronts, warehouses, shops and loading docks, including roll-up and sectional commercial doors and their openers.

    Which areas do you cover?

    Tomball, The Woodlands, Spring, Cypress, Magnolia, Conroe, Klein, Waller, Hockley, Katy, Humble, Atascocita and the rest of north and west Houston.
