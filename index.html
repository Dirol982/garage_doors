    Questions? Answers.

    How fast can you get here?

    Most calls in Tomball, Spring, Cypress, Magnolia and The Woodlands are seen the same day. Emergency calls, like a door stuck open at night or a car trapped inside, are answered 24/7.

    How much is a broken spring?

    Most homes pay $250–$450 to replace both torsion springs. Heavy 18-foot or wood doors cost more. We replace the pair, because the second spring is the same age and usually fails within months.

    Can I open the door with a broken spring?

    Please don't. The spring carries the door's weight, often 150–250 lb. Without it the opener strains and the door can drop. Leave it closed and text us a photo.

    Can you give me a price by text?

    Yes. Text (832) 606-6633 a photo of the whole door from inside, plus one of the springs above it. For most repairs that's enough to name the problem and the price.

    Which brands do you work on?

    All the major door and opener brands found in Texas homes, including LiftMaster, Chamberlain, Genie, Clopay and Amarr, plus older and builder-grade doors.

    Repair it or replace it?

    If the panels are straight and the door is under about 15 years old, repair is usually the better deal. If panels are cracked or rusted through, we'll show you both prices side by side.

    Do I need a wind-rated door?

    Hurricane winds push the garage door in first, and once it fails the roof is next. We install wind-rated doors and can tell you what your builder or HOA requires. Read our guide.

    Do you do commercial doors?

    Yes: storefronts, warehouses, shops and loading docks, including roll-up and sectional commercial doors and their openers.
